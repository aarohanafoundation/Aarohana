Meet Our Team

The passionate individuals driving our mission forward

Our team brings together diverse expertise, innovative ideas, and a shared commitment to creating lasting social impact. We are united by the same goal: to empower communities and build a better future.

Executive Committee

The leadership team guiding our organization

Mohammed Rayeed Zain C A

Founder & President

Meet Mohammed Rayeed Zain, A graduate from St. Joseph's College of Commerce. His mission is simple yet powerful, to bring people together and extend a helping hand to those in need. In these times, when the threads of community often feel frayed, he aims to weave them back together through acts of kindness, support, and collaboration.

Rosanne Pearl Walter

Treasurer

Rosanne is a dedicated youth leader. She is passionate about building partnerships and supporting community-driven initiatives. She actively contributes to fundraising, donor outreach, and event coordination, bringing creativity and commitment to every project. She believes in the power of youth-led change and works towards creating meaningful social impact.

Andrew Joannes

Board Member

Meet Andrew Joannes, A passionate Photographer, Videographer, Editor, and Graphic Designer. With an eye for detail and a love for storytelling, he transforms ideas into visually compelling content that captivates and engages. He’s the kind of creative who shows up on time because inspiration waits for no one, and neither do deadlines.
